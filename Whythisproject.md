###Why I choose this thema
I have been using internet almost 20 years. MIRC, yahoo messenger, friendster and now X, facebook and tiktok. A lot changes, the apps, but also the user. I also realize, that people use social media now to influence other people, also getting information. This can be a problem. Unlike conventional media, that a news is filtered by skilled human, experienced editor, does not apply in social media. People can just share their thoughts, share what they prompt using AI,let it viral. That is why, I am thinking if all of people can equipped themselves with basic logic and critical thinking, much of unnecessary debate, and noise in social media will not happen. I hope technology can bring skill, knowledge, and even wealth democratiazation. What I see now is, opinions are polarized, disinformation and missinformation everywhere, meanwhile skill, knowledge, wealth are only distributed among certain poeple. So, by equipping people with critical thinking, before they use social media, like a driver that should be tested with theoritical question before can actually drive, this app can be a small step to democratize skill, knowledge, and if possible wealth.
